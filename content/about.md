I'm Radu, an engineer and founder, currently Chief Product Architect at
[Akamai](https://www.akamai.com), based in Ghent.

Before Akamai, I co-founded [Fermyon](https://fermyon.com), where I served as
CTO and created [the Spin project](https://github.com/spinframework/spin) — an
open-source framework for building applications with WebAssembly, now a CNCF
project. Akamai acquired Fermyon in January 2026.

Earlier, I was at Microsoft in the
[DeisLabs](https://github.com/deislabs) research and development team, where
I worked on server-side WebAssembly and distributed systems as a core
maintainer for projects like [Krustlet](https://krustlet.dev),
[Draft](https://github.com/Azure/draft),
[Brigade](https://github.com/brigadecore/brigade), and the
[Cloud Native Application Bundle](https://cnab.io). During those years I also
completed Andrew Ng's
[Deep Learning Specialization](https://www.coursera.org/specializations/deep-learning)
and Stanford's
[Machine Learning course](https://www.coursera.org/learn/machine-learning).

Earlier still, in 2015–2016, I volunteered as a mentor with Opening
Opportunities, running Hour of Code workshops and introduction-to-programming
courses for high-school students from underprivileged communities.

Sometimes I write about [serious topics](/blog), and sometimes I post
[shorter updates](/changelog) about projects and experiments.
